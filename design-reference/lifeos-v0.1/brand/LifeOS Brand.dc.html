Spacing

Gap between symbol and wordmark = 0.5× the symbol height. The wordmark's cap-height aligns to the symbol's optical center. A hairline divider is optional, not required.

Clear space

Keep clear space on all sides equal to the diameter of the center dot (≈ 0.34× symbol height). Nothing — type, edges, other marks — enters this zone.

07 — Correct use

Keep the relationship intact

Minimum size 32px on screen, 10mm in print. The six things never to do:

Don't rotate the mark
Don't move the focus point
Don't detach dot from track
Don't substitute colours
Don't add shadows or effects
Don't stretch or distort
